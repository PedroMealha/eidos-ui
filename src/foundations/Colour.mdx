## Text roles

<GuideNote title="Use a text role, never a raw --gray-N" hint="SC 1.4.3">
  The greys are a *surface* ramp. Reaching into them for a label is how the library once ended up
  with 99 declarations of `color: var(--gray-400)` on placeholders, hints and timestamps - `#94a3b8`
  measures 2.56:1 on white, against the 4.5:1 that SC 1.4.3 requires. `--text-disabled` is the one
  deliberate exception: disabled controls are exempt, and are *supposed* to look unavailable.
</GuideNote>

## Palette

Each base is chosen so that white text on it clears 4.5:1 with a little margin - they all sit near
5:1. That headroom is deliberate and thin, which is why muted or faded text on a filled surface does
not work: there is nothing left to spend. Pick a foreground with `--x-contrast` rather than
hardcoding white.
